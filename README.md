## Hey there!

I am a PhD candidate at the Centre for Artificial Intelligence at the Robert Koch Institute; the national public health authority in Germany. I work on machine learning in epidemiology, focussing on vaccine-preventable diseases. While I have worked on air-travel based risk scores associated with infectious diseases, foundation models in case-data predictions, and a range of other topics, my main project has been on implementing Graph Neural Networks in epidemiology (see the repo: **Epi-Spatial-GNN**).

### Background
I hold a BSc in Biophysics (Nanobiology at TU Delft) and a MSc in Infectious Diseases and One Health (Erasmus Mundus Joint Degree). Before starting my PhD, I worked on a large variety of topics. I worked on mRNA quantification in the lab, related to HIV-infections, enterovirus surveillance (both epidemiologically and phylogenetically) and SIR-based modelling.
